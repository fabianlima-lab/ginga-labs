# Phase 2 — holistic blind read: World Cup finals, 1930–2022

From `worldcup_finals.json` (structured factual record — Wikipedia's *List of FIFA
World Cup finals*). This is **DATA** (results), valid for blind inference. Coarse
(finals only) but **holistic and back to 1930**. No narrative consulted.

## The blind signal — goals in the final, by period
- 1930–1970: **5.0**/final · 1974–1986: **4.0** · **1990–2014: 1.4** · 2018–2022: **6.0**

A clear arc: high-scoring finals → a collapse to ~1.4 across **1990–2014** (the
"tight final" era: 1990 1-0, 1994 0-0 pen, 2006 pen, 2010 1-0 aet, 2014 1-0 aet) →
a sharp reversal in 2018 & 2022 (6 each). **9 of 22** finals were unsettled in
regulation or won by ≤1 goal — clustered post-1990.

## Other blind reads
- Titles: Brazil 5, Italy 4, Germany(+WG) 4, Argentina 3, Uruguay/France 2 — a
  South America ↔ Europe duopoly.
- Host won **6 of 22** (1930, 34, 66, 74, 78, 98) — a real home edge, all pre-2000.

## Caveats (important)
- It's only the **final** (n=1/tournament; finals run tighter than group games).
  The proper version is **tournament-wide goals-per-game** — next data pull.
- This is **coarse** data: it surfaces scoring/parity eras, not fine tactical
  eras (possession/pressing), which need event data (2018+).

## What this proves
The reframe is right: **structured factual data is holistic and reaches 1930.** We
*can* build a 1930→now era spine at coarse resolution — and real data-defined eras
live in it (the 1990–2014 tight era and its reversal).
