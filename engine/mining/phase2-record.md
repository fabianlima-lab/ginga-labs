# Phase 2 — consolidated coarse blind read: World Cup 1930–2022

From `worldcup_record.json` (finals + Golden Boot + sourced goals/match extremes).
**Three independent coarse signals, all blind, all holistic back to 1930 — and
they agree.**

## Signal 1 — goals in the final
5.0 (1930–70) → 4.0 (1974–86) → **1.4 (1990–2014)** → 6.0 (2018–22)

## Signal 2 — top-scorer (Golden Boot) goals
8.8 (1930–58, incl. Fontaine **13** in '58) → 7.5 (1962–74) → **6.0 flat
(1978–1998 — exactly 6 for *seven straight* tournaments)** → 6.3 (2002–22)

## Signal 3 — goals per match (sourced record extremes)
1954 = **5.38** (highest ever) … 1990 = **2.21** (lowest ever)

## The data-defined coarse era arc
All three converge: scoring **peaked in the 1950s**, **contracted to a nadir
around 1990**, then **partially reversed** (finals sharply, top-scorers mildly).
The 1978–98 "Golden Boot = 6" plateau and the 1990 goals/match low together mark a
**defensive era ~1978–2002**, bracketed by higher-scoring football.

## Honest limits
- **Coarse:** this is *scoring*, not tactics. It *dates* the defensive era; it
  doesn't *explain* it (Phase 3 + finer data do).
- Full goals/match series not yet pulled — Wikipedia's big table truncates in the
  fetcher; only the two record extremes were sourced (not reconstructed from
  memory). Matches/tournament are format facts.
