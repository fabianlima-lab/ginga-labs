We read the game in the numbers and tell it through the players.

An experiment in whether a machine can truly understand football — not compute it like an outsider, but feel it like the street. Every claim sourced. Every era told through the people who lived it.

Most football data is cold, and most football storytelling is unbacked. We refuse the trade-off.

Every piece here is built the same disciplined way: look at the data first, form a view before reading anyone else's, then check it against the history books — and say plainly where they agree, where the legend is a myth, and where the numbers found something nobody noticed.

And when we're not sure, we say so. That honesty is the whole point.
