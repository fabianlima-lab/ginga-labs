# The analysis model — tendencies as causal chains

Football understanding is *relational*, not a list of facts. Every tendency —
a Canon entry or a live belief — is modeled as a causal chain, never a bare
description:

```
CONTEXT  →  SYSTEM  →  PLAYER PROFILE  →  DATA SIGNATURE  →  EDGE
```

- **Context** — the conditions that make it work: era, opponent block, game
  state, competition, **and environment (heat, altitude, pitch, fatigue)**.
- **System** — the tactical mechanism.
- **Player profile** — the archetype the system needs or exploits.
- **Data signature** — the measurable fingerprint (what shows up in our feeds).
- **Edge** — why it produces an advantage.
- **+ relations** — lineage, counters (what beats it), descendants.

### Worked example — Laranja Mecânica
- context: an era when players held fixed zones
- system: constant positional interchange
- profile: universal players; a Cruyff to conduct
- signature: high rotation + high pass completion + high pressing
- edge: man-marking couldn't track the movement
- relations: beaten by a disciplined zonal block · lineage → Guardiola

## Scope: micro and macro

The same chain works at two levels:

- **MICRO** — one team, now: *"Flamengo's left is exposed in transition."*
  (Belief Bank, tested per match.)
- **MACRO** — the game / an era: *"2026 is the era of the full-back — sides with
  a Hakimi/Kimmich have an edge."* A testable thesis about the state of the game.

### Macro example — "era of the full-back (2026)"
- context: low blocks everywhere; wingers drift inside
- system: full-backs supply width **and** chance creation — the **inverted FB**
  (Kimmich/Cancelo) adds central control, the **overlapping FB** (Hakimi/Theo)
  adds the final ball
- profile: a complete, tactically versatile full-back
- signature: FB share of progressive carries & xA · width-source = FB not winger ·
  inverted-FB central touches
- edge: unlocks compact blocks that smother central play
- test: across WC 2026, do the strong sides over-index on FB attacking output?

### Settled counterpart — "era of the central midfielder (2006)"
The destroyer + metronome pairings (Makelele role crystallizing; Vieira, Pirlo,
Gattuso, Ballack, Essien) — midfield battles decided tournaments. Signature:
midfield share of touches & central progression. This one is **settled** → Canon.

## Lifecycle — macro-beliefs graduate into the Canon

```
observation → belief (micro | macro) → tested → (macro, once settled) → Canon
```

A live era-thesis is a **macro belief**, tested against aggregate data. Once the
era is historical and settled, it graduates into the timeless Canon. "2006 = CM
era" is settled Canon; "2026 = FB era" is a live macro-belief.

## Why this matters

It makes the relationship between **data, systems, players, and conditions**
structural — and it's what lets the agent reason like a football person
("this system needs this kind of player, you'd see it in this number, and it
works because of this context") instead of reciting isolated stats.
