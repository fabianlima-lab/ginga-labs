# Context — the AI-football ecosystem

Not a competitive analysis (see `charter.md`: we're not competing or monetizing).
Just situational awareness — what exists, so we know what we're consuming and
where we sit.

## What's out there

| Player | What it is | Relevance to us |
|--------|------------|-----------------|
| **FootballGPT / aifootball.co** | Marketplace of coaching skills / MCPs / APIs / GPTs. Runs on Claude Desktop, ChatGPT. | Possible **ready-made tools** for our agent's tool layer. Verify their stats aren't just resold API-Football before depending. |
| **FIFA "Football AI Pro"** (FIFA + Lenovo) | Elite assistant for the 48 WC teams; FIFA's Football Language Model, 2,000+ metrics. | Not a competitor — it's *internal to the teams*. It just **confirms the public FIFA EFI data we consume is deep and real.** |
| **Clip-arbitrage bots** | Auto-cut official footage → Shorts → ad revenue. | A cautionary example, not a model — see below. |

## One guardrail for "cool content"

Posting **original generated analysis + graphics** (our threads, pitch/formation
graphics, prediction cards) is our thing: it's ours, it's substantive, it's fun.

**Re-uploading official broadcast footage** (the clip-arbitrage path) is off the
table — copyright/ToS-fragile and it's exactly the soulless AI filler we don't
want to make. We make things; we don't re-cut other people's video.

## Where we sit

We *consume* the existing ecosystem (data feeds, public reports, maybe a tool or
two from the marketplace) and turn it into honest analysis + cool posts. We add
nothing to the data-provider arms race. That's by design.
