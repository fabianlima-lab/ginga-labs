# Landscape & positioning

Not a data layer — this maps the competitive field and where we win. The
AI-football space is in a gold rush (riding World Cup 2026); most of it crowds
two commoditized lanes. Our edge is the third.

## Who's out there

| Player | What it is | Lane |
|--------|------------|------|
| **FootballGPT / aifootball.co** | Marketplace of coaching skills/MCPs/APIs/GPTs (session plans, drills, RefereeGPT). 10k+ coaches. Runs on Claude Desktop/ChatGPT. | **Coaching tools** |
| **FIFA "Football AI Pro"** (FIFA + Lenovo) | Elite assistant for all 48 WC teams; FIFA's Football Language Model, 2,000+ metrics, multi-agent. | **Internal team tool** (not fan-facing) |
| **Clip-arbitrage bots** (e.g. "EasySlice" lore) | Auto-cut official footage → Shorts/Reels → ad revenue, on the tournament calendar. | **Filler content** |

## The two crowded lanes (avoid)

1. **Generic coaching GPTs** — commoditized, marketplace race to the bottom.
2. **Footage-arbitrage filler** — copyright/ToS-fragile, and *exactly* the "AI
   slop" that has no substance. Re-uploading broadcast is off-brand and risky.

## Our lane (the moat)

**Public-facing, tracked, falsifiable analysis + content — `lastreado`.** Every
take is backed by a pre-registered, tested belief with a track record (see
belief-schema). Plus the **no-feed long tail** (Série B, state leagues, várzea)
that no provider — not even FIFA — covers. FIFA Football AI Pro validates the
depth is real and serves *the teams*; we serve *the public conversation* and
the football the data giants ignore.
